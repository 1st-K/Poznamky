Jack told us that after he had read the headlines the night before his throat felt dry so he decided to take a drink. He pointed out that there is always water on his desk when he's reading the news but doesn't usually touch it. But because he was in a hurry he spilt water all down his shirt. His producer warned him not to laugh but once Jack started laughing he couldn't stop. He claims it's the first time anything like that has ever happened to him and promised that it won't happen in tomorrows programme. Jack confessed that he cannot believe that he is the human interest story on his own TV channel.
